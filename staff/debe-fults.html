Debe Fults

Executive Director

Leadership

Debe Fults has been leading CILDRC as Executive Director since 2015, bringing over 20 years of experience in disability services and advocacy. Her vision and leadership have transformed the organization into a leading center for independent living services.

Philosophy

I believe that every person with a disability has the right to live independently and make their own choices. My role is to ensure that CILDRC provides the support, resources, and advocacy needed to make that vision a reality for everyone we serve.

Experience

  • Executive Director, CILDRC (2015 - Present)
  • Program Director, State Disability Services (2010 - 2015)
  • Independent Living Coordinator, Community Services (2005 - 2010)

Key Achievements

  • Expanded CILDRC services to reach 30,000+ individuals annually
  • Secured $2M in grant funding for new programs
  • Established partnerships with 50+ community organizations
  • Led successful advocacy campaigns for accessibility improvements

Education & Certifications

  • Master of Social Work, University of State
  • Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, State University
  • Certified Independent Living Specialist
