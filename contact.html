Common Questions

How do I get started with your services?

Simply contact us by phone, email, or stop by our office. We'll talk with you about your goals and needs, and connect you with the right services. There's no complicated application process.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. You can contact us directly. We also accept referrals from healthcare providers, social workers, family members, and other agencies.

Are your services really free?

Yes, our core services—information and referral, advocacy, independent living skills training, and peer support—are completely free. We never charge for these services.

Is your office accessible?

Absolutely. Our facility is fully accessible with accessible parking, entrance, restrooms, and meeting spaces. We also provide accommodations like sign language interpreters and materials in alternative formats.
