Welcome to the dRC!

The disAbility Resource Center (dRC) is a Center for Independent Living (CIL) serving the Fredericksburg, Virginia, region since 1993. The dRC helps people with disabilities to meet their own, individualized independent living goals.

CIL Core Services

Comprehensive support designed to help people with disabilities meet their individualized independent living goals

Independent Living Skills Training

Our program helps people with disabilities learn new and healthy independent living abilities through hands-on instruction tailored to individual needs.

Individual & Systems Advocacy

We teach consumers how to make their voices heard by assisting people with disabilities to understand their rights and advocate at all levels.

Information & Referral

Services available to anyone in the community with disability-related questions, providing assistance to utilize accessible resources.

Peer Support

Peer mentorship provides personal support and socialization to promote opportunities for successful independent living and family support.

Transition Services

We provide transition services for youth ages 14-24 moving to adult life, and assist people transitioning from institutional settings back to their communities.
