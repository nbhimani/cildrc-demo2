About Us

We are a community-based nonprofit organization led by and for people with disabilities. Our mission is to empower you to live the life you choose.

Our Mission

To promote the full inclusion and participation of people with disabilities in all aspects of community life through advocacy, education, and support services that advance independence and equal opportunity.

Our Vision

A community where people with disabilities have the same opportunities, choices, and freedoms as everyone else—to live where they choose, work at jobs they want, and participate fully in community life.

The Philosophy of Independent Living

Independent living is both a philosophy and a movement

1

Independent living means that people with disabilities have the same civil rights, options, and control over choices in their own lives as do people without disabilities.

2

People with disabilities have the right to live in the community and to participate in the social and economic life of the community.

3

People with disabilities have the right to make choices and decisions about their own lives.

4

People with disabilities are the best experts on their own needs and are capable of making informed decisions about their lives.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do

Consumer Control

People with disabilities are the best experts on their own needs. We put you in charge of your services and support.

Self-Determination

You have the right to make your own choices about where you live, how you live, and what goals you pursue.

Equal Access

Everyone deserves equal access to all aspects of community life, without barriers or discrimination.

Peer Support

People with disabilities helping people with disabilities. Shared experience is powerful and transformative.

What Makes Us Different

Led by People with Disabilities

Our board, staff, and volunteers include people with disabilities. We understand your challenges because we live them too.

Cross-Disability Focus

We serve people with all types of disabilities—physical, sensory, cognitive, and mental health—of all ages.

Community-Based

We're part of your community. Our services are designed to help you live, work, and participate right here.

Free Core Services

Information and referral, advocacy, independent living skills training, and peer support are always free.
