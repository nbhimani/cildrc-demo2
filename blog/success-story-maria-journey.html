Success Story: Maria's Journey to Independence

March 10, 2025
4 min read
Seth Whitten, Deputy Director

Maria Rodriguez's story is one of determination, resilience, and the transformative power of support and community. Today, she's not only living independently but also mentoring others on their journey to independence.

The Beginning

Three years ago, Maria came to CILDRC feeling uncertain about her future. After a car accident left her with a spinal cord injury, she struggled with the transition from hospital to home and felt overwhelmed by the challenges ahead.

"I didn't know where to start. Everything felt impossible. I couldn't imagine living on my own again."

Finding Support

Through our Independent Living Skills Training program, Maria began working with a peer mentor who had faced similar challenges. Together, they developed a personalized plan focusing on:

  • Adaptive daily living skills
  • Home modifications and accessibility
  • Transportation solutions
  • Employment preparation

Building Confidence

Over the next year, Maria participated in various programs at CILDRC. She learned to use assistive technology, attended peer support groups, and worked with our employment services team to update her resume and practice interview skills.

"The peer support groups were life-changing," Maria shares. "Meeting others who understood what I was going through made me realize I wasn't alone. Their success stories gave me hope."

Achieving Independence

Today, Maria lives independently in an accessible apartment, works as a customer service representative for a local company, and volunteers as a peer mentor at CILDRC.

"I want to be for others what my mentor was for me – proof that independence is possible and that you don't have to do it alone."

Giving Back

As a peer mentor, Maria has helped over a dozen individuals navigate their own journeys to independence. She leads support groups, speaks at community events, and advocates for accessibility in local businesses.

"CILDRC didn't just help me learn skills," Maria reflects. "They helped me rediscover who I am and what I'm capable of. Now I get to help others do the same."

Your Journey Starts Here

Maria's story is just one of thousands we've witnessed over our 40 years of service. If you're facing challenges with independent living, know that support is available and independence is possible.

Contact us today to learn about our programs and connect with peer mentors who understand your journey.
