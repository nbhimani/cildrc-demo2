      <p>Assistive technology continues to evolve rapidly, offering new solutions that enhance independence and accessibility. Here are the top 10 assistive technologies making a difference in 2025.</p>

      <h2>1. AI-Powered Screen Readers</h2>
      <p>The latest screen readers use artificial intelligence to provide more natural, context-aware descriptions of visual content, making web browsing and app usage more intuitive for people with visual impairments.</p>

      <h2>2. Smart Home Accessibility Systems</h2>
      <p>Integrated smart home systems now offer voice control, automated accessibility features, and customizable interfaces that adapt to individual needs and preferences.</p>

      <h2>3. Advanced Mobility Devices</h2>
      <p>New wheelchair technologies include AI-assisted navigation, terrain adaptation, and standing capabilities, providing greater independence and versatility.</p>

      <h2>4. Communication Apps</h2>
      <p>AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) apps have become more sophisticated, offering predictive text, natural-sounding voices, and seamless integration with other devices.</p>

      <h2>5. Wearable Health Monitors</h2>
      <p>Accessible wearable devices now track vital signs, medication schedules, and provide emergency alerts with features designed specifically for people with disabilities.</p>

      <h2>6. Adaptive Gaming Controllers</h2>
      <p>Gaming has become more accessible with customizable controllers and adaptive devices that allow people with limited mobility to enjoy video games.</p>

      <h2>7. Real-Time Captioning Glasses</h2>
      <p>Smart glasses that provide real-time captions of conversations and environmental sounds are revolutionizing communication for people who are deaf or hard of hearing.</p>

      <h2>8. Accessible E-Readers</h2>
      <p>E-readers with enhanced accessibility features including adjustable fonts, text-to-speech, and tactile feedback make reading more accessible than ever.</p>

      <h2>9. Medication Management Systems</h2>
      <p>Smart pill dispensers with reminders, tracking, and caregiver notifications help ensure medication adherence and safety.</p>

      <h2>10. Virtual Reality Therapy Tools</h2>
      <p>VR technology is being used for physical therapy, cognitive training, and mental health support with accessible interfaces and adaptive controls.</p>

      <h2>Getting Started with Assistive Technology</h2>
      <p>At CILDRC, we offer assistive technology assessments, demonstrations, and training. Our equipment lending library allows you to try devices before purchasing, and our staff can help you navigate funding options.</p>

      <p>Contact us to schedule an assistive technology consultation and discover which tools might work best for you.</p>
